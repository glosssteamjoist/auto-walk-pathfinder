## 📖 About

Auto-Walk Pathfinder navigates your character to a target coordinate automatically, pathfinding around obstacles, across water, and up cliffs without input.

---

## ✨ Features
<a name="features"></a>

- **Coordinate Target** — type `/goto X Y Z` and walk away.
- **Obstacle Avoidance** — routes around walls, water, and cliffs.
- **Sprint & Jump** — uses sprinting and jumping for speed.
- **Stop Conditions** — pauses on fall damage risk or unexpected entity.
- **Progress HUD** — distance remaining shown in HUD.
- **Cancel Key** — instant stop on keypress.

> 🔑 **100% free** — no key system, no linkvertise, no ads.
